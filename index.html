Tell us about your innovation and the support you need

This short questionnaire helps the NIHR Research Support Service understand your innovation, its stage of development and where academic or funding support may add value.

What happens after you submit?

Your responses will be reviewed by the RSS team. If your innovation meets the criteria for our support, we will arrange a meeting with you to discuss your needs and how the RSS team can best support you. Where appropriate, we may also signpost you to relevant funding or partner organisations.

How long will it take?

Around 10–15 minutes. You do not need to have all the answers at this stage — tell us what you know and indicate where things are still being developed.

2. Your innovation and readiness

These questions help the RSS team understand what you are developing, how far it has progressed and whether there is a clear opportunity for further evidence or research.

Please select the level that best reflects your current stage. If you are unsure, choose “Not sure” and the RSS team can discuss this with you. The descriptions are based on the KTH Innovation Technology Readiness Level (TRL) guidance.

3. Market and need

We want to understand whether there is evidence of a genuine need for the innovation and whether the market or intended users have been considered.

Please avoid confidential or sensitive personal information. If you are unsure whether something should be included, leave it out and discuss it with the RSS team.
